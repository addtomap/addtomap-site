Frequently asked

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. Download a tour once (Wi-Fi recommended, 30–80 MB per tour) and everything — audio, texts, photos and the map — works offline. Downloaded tours stay on the device until you remove them.

The tour doesn't download

Check that you are online and have a few hundred MB free, then open the tour page again — downloads resume where they stopped. If it keeps failing, write to us with the tour name.

Location and the "Start tour" button

Guidance along the route needs the location permission (iOS Settings → AddToMap → Location → While Using). Without it you can still listen to every stop in preview mode.

Purchases, restore and refunds

Paid tours and city packs are one-time purchases tied to your Apple ID — no subscription. On a new or reinstalled device use Settings → Restore purchases inside the app. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Deleting your data

There is no account. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device; see the Privacy Policy for what little we collect.

Feedback

Rate a tour at its end or on the tour page — low ratings open a short private note to us, and we read every one. Ideas for new cities are welcome by email.

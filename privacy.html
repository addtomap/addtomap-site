Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

AddToMap ("we", "the app") provides offline, self-guided audio walking tours. We built the app to need as little of your data as possible. This policy explains what we do and don't collect.

What we do NOT collect

  • No account, no sign-up, no name, email or phone number.
  • No advertising, no ad identifiers (IDFA), no cross-app/-site tracking.
  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your location on your device to show where you are on the map and guide you along a tour route. Your location is not sent to us or stored on our servers. You can use tours in a preview mode without location, and you can revoke the permission anytime in iOS Settings.

Anonymous diagnostics

To fix bugs and understand which features are used, the app collects anonymous analytics and crash reports via Google Firebase (Analytics without advertising identifiers, and Crashlytics). This includes app events (e.g. a tour was opened), crash/error information, device model, OS version, app version and coarse region — tied only to a random install identifier, not to your identity. It is used solely to improve the app, never for advertising or tracking.

Tour content delivery

Tour files (audio, text, images) are downloaded from our content delivery network (cdn.addtomap.co, served via Cloudflare). Standard technical request data (such as IP address) may be processed by Cloudflare to deliver the files and protect the service; we do not use it to identify you.

Processors

  • Google Firebase (Analytics / Crashlytics) — diagnostics.
  • Cloudflare — content delivery and security.
  • Apple — app distribution and, for paid tours, payment (Apple is the seller of record).

Your rights (GDPR)

As the data is anonymous we generally cannot link it to you, but you can stop all diagnostics by deleting the app, and you control location in iOS Settings. For any question or request, contact us.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date reflects the latest version.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

AddToMap ("we", "the app") provides offline, self-guided audio walking tours. We built the app to need as little of your data as possible. This policy explains what we do and don't collect.

Who is responsible

Controller under the GDPR: Anton Tonkushin, Rödelheimer Landstraße 5, 60487 Frankfurt am Main, Germany, support@addtomap.co (see also the Impressum).

What we do NOT collect

  • No account, no sign-up, no name, email or phone number.
  • No advertising, no ad identifiers (IDFA), no cross-app/-site tracking.
  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your location on your device to show where you are on the map and guide you along a tour route. Your location is not sent to us or stored on our servers. You can use tours in a preview mode without location, and you can revoke the permission anytime in iOS Settings.

Anonymous diagnostics

To fix bugs and understand which features are used, the app collects anonymous analytics and crash reports via Google Firebase (Analytics without advertising identifiers, and Crashlytics). This includes app events (e.g. a tour was opened), crash/error information, device model, OS version, app version and coarse region — tied only to a random install identifier, not to your identity. It is used solely to improve the app, never for advertising or tracking.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in a stable app and in knowing which features are used. Retention: crash reports are kept for 90 days; analytics events for at most 14 months (Google's retention setting), then deleted automatically.

Ratings and feedback

After a tour the app may ask you to rate it from 1 to 5 stars. Ratings are stored anonymously as a per-tour counter (sum and number of ratings) in Google Firebase Firestore — nothing in them identifies you. If you choose to tell us what could be better, that text (with the rating, the tour, the app language and version — no identifier) is stored in Firestore and forwarded to us through a Cloudflare Worker to a Telegram chat so we can read it quickly. It is never shown in the app or published; please don't include personal data in it. With 4–5 stars the app shows Apple's standard App Store review prompt, which is handled entirely by Apple.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (improving the tours) and, for the text you choose to send, your decision to send it. Ratings are kept as counters while the tour is offered; feedback texts are deleted once they have been read and acted on.

Tour content delivery

Tour files (audio, text, images) are downloaded from our content delivery network (cdn.addtomap.co, served via Cloudflare). Standard technical request data (such as IP address) may be processed by Cloudflare to deliver the files and protect the service; we do not use it to identify you. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — delivering the tours you asked for — and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for security.

This website

addtomap.co is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. When you open it, the servers process technical request data (IP address, time, requested page, browser) to deliver the page and protect it against attacks; the providers keep such logs only briefly. The site uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party content. Your chosen language and preview voice are saved in your browser's local storage only on your device. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Purchases

Paid tours are bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment as the seller; we never receive your payment details or your Apple ID — the app only learns which tours are unlocked.

Processors

  • Google Firebase (Analytics / Crashlytics / Firestore) — diagnostics, anonymous tour counters and feedback.
  • Cloudflare — content delivery, security and the feedback relay (Workers).
  • Telegram — delivery of the feedback you choose to send, to the developer's chat.
  • Apple — app distribution and, for paid tours, payment (Apple is the seller of record).
  • GitHub — hosting of this website.

Google, Cloudflare and GitHub may process data in the USA; they are certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision of the European Commission, Art. 45 GDPR). Feedback relayed to Telegram may be processed outside the EU — please do not include personal data in it.

Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). As the data is anonymous we generally cannot link it to you; you can stop all diagnostics by deleting the app, and you control location in iOS Settings. Write to us for any request.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example in your country of residence. The authority responsible for us is the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (HBDI), Gustav-Stresemann-Ring 1, 65189 Wiesbaden, Germany.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date reflects the latest version.

Contact

Data controller: the person named in the Impressum.
